Research Profile & Mission

The Chapagain Laboratory focuses on integrating plant breeding, crop stress physiology, and plant pathology to address emerging challenges in global food security. Our team utilizes high-throughput field phenotyping tools—such as NDVI spectral indices and canopy thermal imaging—alongside molecular diagnostic assays to evaluate biotic and abiotic stress responses in staple crops. Through multi-environment evaluations, we aim to accelerate the development of disease-resistant, drought-tolerant crop cultivars.
