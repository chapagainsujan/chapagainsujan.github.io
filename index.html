I am an Agricultural Researcher specializing in Agronomy, Plant Pathology, and Plant Breeding. My work focuses on integrating field phenotyping, stress physiology, multivariate statistics, and reproducible data workflows in R and Python to enhance crop productivity and resilience.

My research experience includes evaluating drought physiological responses in cereal crops using sensors (NDVI, canopy temperature), assessing botanical extracts against fungal pathogens, and analyzing genetic diversity across landraces using linear mixed-effects models (REML/BLUP) and selection indices.
